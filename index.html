What is Zoho Inventory WooCommerce Integration?

Zoho Inventory WooCommerce integration connects your online store with Zoho Inventory, allowing automatic synchronization of products, orders, and stock levels between both platforms.

Whenever a customer places an order on WooCommerce, inventory updates automatically in Zoho Inventory. Product or stock changes also reflect instantly on your store.

The result is better accuracy, improved efficiency, and a seamless operational workflow.
